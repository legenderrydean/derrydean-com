Heritors Labs

Founder & Principal Innovation Architect, 2019–present · heritorslabs.com ↗
Active

The platform through which I now operate across Ghana and the United States, spanning technology platforms, policy consulting, and research commercialisation. As Principal Investigator, secured USD 1.09M across two phases of the UK FCDO's RISA Fund, one of the largest competitive research commercialisation grants awarded to an individual African-led firm, directing seven national-level policy frameworks across five African countries. Client Engagements

Contracted, and delivered, by name.

MTN Ghana (Scancom PLC)

Channel Partner Business Transformation Programme
Aug 2024 – Jan 2025 · GHS 2,525,951 contract

Contracted under a formal Consultancy Services Agreement to lead a comprehensive business transformation programme across MTN's nine Channel Partners: the network distributing MTN's full product portfolio across Ghana's largest mobile carrier (70%+ national market share). Deliverables included a Business Profitability Framework, a Paradigm Assessment Report, IMVCM-based Value Chain Mapping, Business Model Canvases, a Change Management Blueprint, a Future Readiness Score, and a Digital Skills Manual.

GIZ Germany

Ghana's First National AI Practitioners' Guide
Nov 2024 – Oct 2025 · GHS 1,475,800 contract

Team Lead under GIZ's FAIR Forward global programme, responsible for drafting Ghana's first AI Practitioners' Guide: preparatory research, stakeholder mapping, multi-stakeholder workshops, expert pool development, and the final guide translating national AI strategy into deployable methodology for government, regulators, and the private sector.

GFA Consulting Group (Germany) / BMZ

Digital Transformation for Informal MSMEs
Oct 2023 – present

Senior Technical Expert on a German Federal Ministry (BMZ)-funded programme, directing human-centric service design and digital experience strategy for women micro-entrepreneurs. Delivered user testing across 12+ digital apps for informal MSMEs, designed digital solution provider recruitment criteria, and built training and engagement frameworks.

The Future Society, Harvard Kennedy School

Ghana's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2022–2033
Dec 2021 – Oct 2022

Contracted as Digital Policy and Legal Expert to co-develop Ghana's National AI Strategy, including stakeholder engagement, policy gap analysis, and drafting regulatory frameworks in collaboration with GIZ FAIR Forward and Ghana's Ministry of Communications.

UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (via Chemonics)

RISA Fund: Research and Innovation Systems for Africa
2021 – 2025 · USD 1.29M cumulative grants

Lead grantee through Heritors Labs across the early phase and the large-scale Phase I & II expansion of FCDO's RISA Fund, plus an Accountable Grant Agreement for the UK–Africa Innovation Trust Corridor research. Scope included founding the Africa Research and Innovation Commercialisation Summits (ARICS), developing the IMVCM framework, and multi-country research-to-market support.

United Nations Capital Development Fund (UNCDF)

Ghana Cashlite Roadmap Implementation
Nov 2019 – Nov 2020 · USD 91,400

Contracted by name as sole individual expert to lead Ghana's national journey to a cashless economy, under the Office of the Vice President. Designed the Digital Payments Coordination Unit structure, developed Cabinet papers on Digital Financial Services, and coordinated between the Bank of Ghana, the Ministry of Finance, and international development partners.

Ministry of Communications, Digital Technology & Innovation, Ghana

Digital Economy Study Coordinator, Ghana Digital Acceleration Project
Jul – Oct 2023

Coordinated Ghana's Digital Economy Studies across agriculture, education, entrepreneurship, public service, and connectivity sectors, supervising data collection and stakeholder coordination and producing the final packaged report for the Ministry and the World Bank.

Volta River Authority

Enterprise Digital Transformation Programme
Founding Principal & Chief Advisor, The Heritors People

Named Lead Consultant directing a three-deliverable enterprise transformation programme for Ghana's primary power generation authority: a 17-department State of Digital Affairs baseline assessment, a five-year Digital Transformation and Innovation Blueprint (2023–2028), and the design of VRA's Digital Transformation and Innovation Centre.
