Work With Me

Five ways to engage. Pick the one that fits.

A short strategy call is one way in, not the only one. Most of the work is consulting projects, retained advisory, board seats, and government or multilateral programmes, engagements that are scoped and billed on their own terms rather than booked off a price list. If you already have a request for proposals, terms of reference, or a written brief, submit it directly and skip the back-and-forth.

Option 1: Fixed-Fee Sessions

Pick a format, send a message, and you'll receive a secure payment and scheduling link within 24 hours.

The only engagement type that's pre-priced and self-serve. Everything else below is scoped individually, see the models further down the page.

Option 2–5: Scoped Engagements

These are billed on their own terms, not off a price list.

Each of these is scoped through a conversation or a written brief before any fee is agreed. If you already have the paperwork, the fastest route is the submission form below.

Option 2

Consulting & Project Engagements

Defined-scope work: architecture reviews, strategy design, ecosystem diagnostics, product or policy build-outs.

Billed: day rate from $1,500/day, or a fixed project fee built from that rate, set out in a written proposal after scoping.

Option 3

Retained Advisory

Ongoing counsel for founders, executive teams, or ministries, on call across a running set of decisions rather than one session.

Billed: monthly retainer from $6,000/month (a four-day-equivalent floor at the $1,500 day rate), invoiced in advance.

Option 4

Board & Governance

Fintech and digital infrastructure board seats, advisory board membership, governance design.

Billed: individually negotiated, cash, equity, or a hybrid, under a separate board agreement.

Option 5

Government & Multilateral Programmes

National strategy design, policy architecture, and programme delivery, usually via a competitive RFP or tender process.

Billed: per the contracting authority's procurement terms, under a formal consultancy agreement.

Also available

Speaking Engagements

Keynotes, panels, and moderated sessions on digital payments architecture, national AI and digital policy, and innovation ecosystems.

Billed: fee varies by event, format, and travel.
