Capabilities

What you get when you bring me in.

I work at the point where product, policy, and capital meet: the layer that determines whether a platform scales, a regulation permits, and a national system actually functions. That is a deliberate position, built by working across all three, not a byproduct of staying in one lane. Below is the capability set, organised by the outcome it produces, not the buzzword attached to it.

01 · Architecture

Digital & Financial Infrastructure Architecture

For central banks, regulators, telcos, and fintech platforms that need the plumbing to actually hold under scale, fraud pressure, and cross-border complexity.

  • Payment systems architecture and interoperability standards design
  • Regulatory sandbox policy and licensing regime design (built from direct work on Ghana's Payment Systems and Services Act)
  • Bilateral financial interoperability frameworks (chaired the Technology Track of the Ghana–Singapore Financial Trust Corridor with MAS and the Bank of Ghana)
  • Fintech platform architecture, from first-principles product design through multinational deployment
  • Central bank digital currency and RegTech/compliance framework advisory
02 · Policy

Policy Design & National AI Governance

For governments, ministries, and multilateral programmes that need a policy instrument designed to survive contact with implementation, not a slide deck.

  • Policy design and drafting, from first-principles problem framing through legislative or cabinet-ready instrument
  • National AI strategy design, multi-stakeholder consultation, and 10-year implementation roadmaps
  • Cabinet-level policy documentation and digital economy programme direction
  • National digital transformation blueprints designed for cross-institutional interoperability, not single-ministry silos (designated Principal Architect of the Ghana Integrated Digital Transformation Blueprint)
  • Responsible AI implementation methodology, translating strategy into deployable practice for regulators and the private sector
  • Standardisation and conformity assessment charters aligned to ISO and international market-readiness requirements
03 · Ecosystem

Innovation & Research Commercialisation

For research institutions, DFIs, and innovation agencies trying to close the gap between what researchers invent and what markets will actually pay for.

  • The Innovation Market Value Chain Map (IMVCM): a 14-node diagnostic protocol across five phases for identifying the structural gaps preventing a technology from reaching commercial scale. Full detail available on request while the public breakdown is finalised; the originating research is published on ResearchGate ↗.
  • RISCAM (Research Innovation Socialisation, Communication and Marketing): the operational framework for translating technical research into investable propositions
  • Cross-border research-to-market exchange frameworks (the Innovation Trust Corridor model)
  • Triple Helix (research–industry–university) governance architecture design
  • Grant strategy and competitive funding acquisition: track record includes USD 1.4M+ secured as named Principal Investigator
04 · Advisory

Board, Venture & Executive Advisory

For founders and boards who need a director or advisor who has actually built, not only studied, the categories they operate in.

  • Fintech and digital infrastructure board advisory
  • Public–private partnership design and multilateral programme structuring
  • Change management and institutional transformation for large distribution networks (led a business transformation programme across MTN Ghana's nine Channel Partners)
  • Venture strategy, product architecture review, and go-to-market sequencing for early-stage founders
05 · Product

Product & Platform Development Strategy

For teams that need direction from someone who has actually shipped the product, not only advised around it: the policy and architecture work above is grounded in having built the platforms it governs.

  • Human-centred UX and digital experience strategy (GFA Consulting Group/BMZ programme: directed user testing across 12+ digital apps for informal MSMEs)
  • Platform development direction and product strategy for GGTI's digital tooling
  • Product architecture across the ventures built and directed personally, spanning Slydepay, SMARTQORE, HostelHaus, and the wider Heritors platform suite
  • Technical fluency that keeps policy and architecture recommendations grounded in what is actually buildable, not only what is theoretically sound
